Gladiator vs. Wrangler JLU Seat Covers — What’s Different

This is the most common mistake Gladiator owners make:

  • Front seats: The Gladiator JT front seats are nearly identical to the JLU 4-door front seats. Front-only covers from JLU sources may fit, but always confirm with the seller.
  • Rear seats: Completely different. The Gladiator rear seats face forward in a truck cab layout — different width, height, and positioning from the JLU rear bench. JLU rear covers will not fit a Gladiator correctly.
  • The safe bet: Always order covers specifically listed as "fits Jeep Gladiator JT 2020+" for the rear — not "fits JL/JLU."

Front covers may fit since the seat geometry is similar. Rear covers will not — the Gladiator truck rear cab is a completely different shape from the JLU rear bench. Always order Gladiator-specific rear covers.

Potentially yes. The Mojave edition has different front seats from other Gladiator trims. Verify Mojave-specific fitment before ordering — Bartact is the most likely source for a confirmed Mojave fit.
